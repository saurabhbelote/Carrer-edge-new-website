Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, pricing, and processes.

Category 1

Resume Writing

What is the typical turnaround time for resume writing?

Our turnaround times vary by plan: Basic plan - 7 days, Pro plan - 3 days, Ultimate plan - 24-48 hours. We also offer rush delivery options for urgent needs at an additional cost.

How many revisions are included?

The Basic plan includes 1 round of revisions. Pro and Ultimate plans typically include more comprehensive revision support until you are satisfied.

What makes your resumes ATS-optimized?

We use industry-standard formatting, strategic keyword placement, proper section headers, and avoid graphics/tables that confuse ATS systems. Our expert writers understand how ATS algorithms scan and rank resumes.

How are pricing plans determined?

Pricing is based on the level of service, turnaround time, and included features. Our Basic plan begins at ₹499, Pro at ₹699, and Ultimate at ₹1,199.
Category 2

LinkedIn Optimization

How does LinkedIn optimization improve profile visibility?

We optimize your headline, summary, and experience sections with relevant keywords, improve your profile completeness score, and implement strategies that make your profile more attractive to LinkedIn's algorithm and recruiters.

Will recruiters really notice my profile more?

Yes! An optimized LinkedIn profile can increase your profile views by 3-5x. We use proven techniques to improve your search ranking and make your profile stand out to recruiters actively searching for candidates like you.

What is included in the LinkedIn optimization service?

Complete profile audit, keyword-optimized headline and summary, experience section enhancement, skills optimization, and recommendations for profile photo and background image. We also provide guidance on content strategy.

Interview Preparation

What does interview preparation coaching include?

Our coaching includes one-on-one sessions with industry experts, mock interviews tailored to your target role, detailed feedback on your responses, body language tips, and strategies for handling difficult questions.

What is your success rate with interview coaching?

Over 85% of our clients who complete our interview preparation program successfully clear their interviews and receive job offers. We focus on building genuine confidence and providing practical, actionable feedback.

Sessions are conducted via video call for convenience and flexibility. Each session is 60 minutes long and can be scheduled based on your availability. We also provide recorded sessions for your review.
